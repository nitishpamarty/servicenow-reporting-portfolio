Reporting Analyst Portfolio

A working demo of ITSM reporting built the way a Reporting Analyst role expects it: native ServiceNow Performance Analytics dashboards and homepages, a companion Tableau dashboard, and findings translated into recommendations — not just charts.

⚠ All data is synthetic, generated on a personal ServiceNow Developer Instance (PDI) — not real client or production data.

03 — Findings & Recommendations

What the data shows

The point of a reporting analyst isn't the dashboard — it's what you do with it. A couple of examples of translating the synthetic data into a recommendation:

  • Finding

    Placeholder — e.g. "38% of Priority 2 incidents over the trailing 90 days trace back to a single business application, well above its share of overall ticket volume." Recommendation: prioritize a problem-management review of that application's stability before the next release window.

  • Finding

    Placeholder — e.g. "12% of incident records were missing category or assignment group at close, undermining trend reporting." Recommendation: enforce required fields at state transition and flag a CMDB data-quality sweep.
